# TSMR-F extension — De Aquino threshold, pentagonal half-norm, and golden Bernoulli resonance

**Record type:** cross-domain audit / proposed connection  
**Status:** exact algebraic identities are recorded separately from model-dependent and conjectural claims. This document does not assert that De Aquino's physical proposal is experimentally established, nor that the three occurrences of (1/2) are the same quantity.

## 1. Three distinct meanings of one-half

The project contains at least three mathematically different appearances of (1/2). They must not be conflated.

### 1.1 De Aquino's zero-gravitational-mass threshold

In De Aquino's 2002 model, write

[
\frac{m_g}{m_i}=3-2\sqrt{1+\xi^2},\qquad
\xi=\frac{\Delta p}{m_i c}=\frac{n_r U}{m_i c^2}.
]

Setting the model's factor (m_g/m_i) equal to zero gives

[
3-2\sqrt{1+\xi_*^2}=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\xi_*^2=\frac54,
\qquad |\xi_*|=\frac{\sqrt5}{2}.
]

Since (\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2),

[
\boxed{|\xi_*|=\varphi-\frac12},
\qquad
\boxed{\varphi=\frac12+|\xi_*|}.
]

This is an exact algebraic identity **inside the stated De Aquino model**. It does not independently validate the proposed gravitational/inertial-mass relation or its physical interpretation. Here (U) denotes absorbed/emitted energy in the model; it must not be silently replaced by an energy density.

### 1.2 The pentagonal-frame half projection

For the regular pentagonal frame used in the RGS/TSMR notes, let (E\in\mathbb R^{2\times5}) contain the five unit vectors at angles (2\pi k/5). Then

[
EE^T=\frac52 I_2,\qquad G_5=E^TE,\qquad
P=\frac25G_5.
]

The matrix (P) is the orthogonal projector onto the two-dimensional row space of (E). For a unit vector (q\in\mathbb R^5),

[
q^TG_5q=\frac54
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\|Eq\|^2=\frac54
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\boxed{\|Pq\|^2=\frac12}.
]

Thus the level set on the unit sphere is
[
S^1(1/\sqrt2)\times S^2(1/\sqrt2)
\subset S^4
]
after the orthogonal splitting into the rank-two image and rank-three kernel of (P).

This (1/2) is a **squared projection norm** arising from the regular pentagonal frame. It is not the Bernoulli probability, not the contraction ratio, and not by itself a consequence of De Aquino's equation. The exact algebraic bridge to De Aquino is instead the separate identity (\varphi=1/2+|\xi_*|). Any stronger identification requires an independently stated map between the physical parameter and the pentagonal-frame variable.

### 1.3 Bernoulli probability and geometric contraction

For Bernoulli convolutions, distinguish:
- (p=1/2): equal probabilities of the two symbols;
- (\lambda=1/2): geometric contraction ratio at the interval/Cantor support transition.

These are independent parameters. In the symmetric Bernoulli convolution with contraction (\lambda=1/2), the distribution is uniform on the interval ([-2,2]) for the convention (X=\sum_{n\ge0}\epsilon_n\lambda^n), (\epsilon_n\in\{-1,+1\}).

## 2. Golden-ratio resonance and symbolic collisions

Set
[
\lambda=\varphi^{-1}=\frac{\sqrt5-1}{2},\qquad
\lambda^2+\lambda=1.
]
For binary coding (Y=\sum_{k\ge0}d_k\lambda^k), (d_k\in\{0,1\}), the relation (1=\lambda+\lambda^2) gives the first collision:
[
(1,0,0)\ne(0,1,1),\qquad
1=\lambda+\lambda^2.
]
This is an exact collision of distinct finite symbolic words under the geometric coding map. It is the elementary manifestation of the Fibonacci algebra underlying the golden Bernoulli convolution.

The collision does **not** mean that every finite-level count is a Fibonacci number. Direct exact arithmetic in (\mathbb Q(\lambda)), representing (a+b\lambda) by the integer pair ((a,b)) and reducing (\lambda^2=1-\lambda), gives the following numbers of distinct sums for the first levels:

| Word length (n) | Distinct sums |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 2 |
| 2 | 4 |
| 3 | 7 |
| 4 | 12 |
| 5 | 20 |

In particular, the claim that these counts are (F_{n+2}), and the earlier claim of 11 distinct sums at (n=4), are not valid for this finite-sum convention. Fibonacci structures arise in the appropriate overlap/transition graph, but must not be substituted for this raw distinct-value count.

## 3. Entropy and dimension: what is established

Let (\nu_\lambda) be the equal-weight Bernoulli convolution generated by (\sum_{n\ge0}\epsilon_n\lambda^n). For the golden parameter (\lambda=\varphi^{-1}), the Pisot property produces systematic overlaps and the measure is singular. The dimension is strictly below one and is numerically about

[
\dim_H\nu_{\varphi^{-1}}\approx0.995713ldots
]

The classical numerical estimate is due to J. C. Alexander and D. Zagier (1991), not “Alexander–Yorke (1984)”. Alexander and Yorke supplied earlier connections with information dimension/fat baker attractors; Alexander–Zagier gave the high-precision golden-mean entropy estimate. Modern surveys report the rigorous bracket
[
0.99557<\dim_H\nu_{\varphi^{-1}}<0.99574.
]
Do not present 0.995713... as an exact closed form.

The formula connecting dimension and Garsia entropy in the Pisot case is convention-dependent. With (h_\lambda) denoting the entropy per symbolic step in natural logarithms,
[
\dim_H\nu_\lambda=\frac{h_\lambda}{|\log\lambda|}
\]
for the relevant Pisot Bernoulli convolution. If instead a normalized entropy (H_\lambda=h_\lambda/|\log\lambda|) is used, then (H_\lambda) itself is the dimension. State the convention whenever quoting a numerical entropy.

## 4. TSMR-F extension: symbolic-to-geometric entropy loss

The TSMR-F framework should therefore retain the coding map as a separate layer:
[
\pi_\lambda:\{0,1\}^{\mathbb N}\to\mathbb R,
\qquad
\pi_\lambda(d)=\sum_{n\ge0}d_n\lambda^n.
]
The Bernoulli measure on symbol space is pushed forward by (\pi_\lambda). Distinct symbolic sequences may have the same image. The resulting entropy loss is a property of the coding factor, not merely of the visible support.

A useful research object is the finite-level equivalence relation
[
u\sim_{\lambda,n}v
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(u_k-v_k)\lambda^k=0,
]
together with the multiplicities
[
m_{\lambda,n}(x)=\#\{u\in\{0,1\}^n:\pi_{\lambda,n}(u)=x\}.
]
The finite-level Shannon entropy is
[
H_n(\lambda)=-\sum_x
\frac{m_{\lambda,n}(x)}{2^n}
\log\frac{m_{\lambda,n}(x)}{2^n}.
]
Its asymptotic normalization must be matched to the chosen definition of Garsia entropy before computing dimensions.

For the vesica programme, this suggests testing whether anisotropic recursive maps create exact or near symbolic collisions. That is a proposed extension only: no such collision law has yet been established for the vesica IFS.

## 5. Research questions and classification

1. Can the RGS pentagonal projector (P) be coupled to a symbolic coding map so that its half-projection condition has a derived probabilistic interpretation? **Open; no identification assumed.**
2. Can the De Aquino dimensionless threshold (\xi_*=\sqrt5/2) be mapped to a normalized RGS/TSMR variable without changing its physical meaning? **Open; the identity (\varphi=1/2+\xi_*) alone is insufficient.**
3. Do the anisotropic vesica maps admit a finite overlap automaton analogous to the golden Bernoulli/Fibonacci graph? **Candidate computational problem.**
4. Does the induced measure have a nontrivial (D_q) spectrum after exact overlap multiplicities are accounted for? **To be tested.**

**Classification:** the De Aquino threshold algebra, pentagonal-frame projector identity, golden polynomial relation, and first symbolic collision are exact conditional/algebraic statements. The golden Bernoulli singularity and dimension estimate are established literature results. A common physical origin of these structures, or a unifying RGS/De Aquino theorem, is not established.
