# T143 — antipodal connection and holonomy

**Two distinct evidence tracks are maintained here.**

- The reconstructed-embedding audit is documented below and in
  \`scripts/audit_t143_antipodal_connection.py\`.
- The direct replay of the original archived link matrices is documented in
  [the original-archive replay record](T143-original-archived-connection-replay.md)
  and implemented in
  \`scripts/audit_t143_original_archived_connection.py\`.

The exact mathematical result is now stated separately in
[T143-A — Antipodal equivariance of the minimal SO(3)/SU(2) connection](../theorems/T143A-antipodal-equivariance-minimal-so3-su2-connection.md).
It proves link, path, cycle and quotient-connection equivariance under
explicit hypotheses. It does **not** by itself establish that the original
floating-point archive was generated by those formulas.

## 1. Reconstructed geometric antipode

The source archive supplies \`X_Oh_reconstructed\` and \`Q6_adjacency\`.
Recompute
\[
p(g)=\arg\min_h\|X_h+X_g\|.
\]
It agrees exactly with \(p(g)=g\oplus7\); the antipodal residual is zero,
and XOR 7 is an exact Q6 adjacency automorphism.

## 2. Reconstructed link transport

Normalize \(n_g=X_g/\|X_g\|\) and let \(R_{gh}\) be the minimal rotation
carrying \(n_g\) to \(n_h\). Its formula is
\[
R_{gh}=I+[n_g\times n_h]_\times+
\frac{[n_g\times n_h]_\times^2}{1+n_g\cdot n_h}.
\]
The reconstructed directed transports satisfy
\(R_{p(g)p(h)}=R_{gh}\). The structural reason is that both the dot
product and cross product are unchanged when both normals change sign.

For the canonical spin lift
\[
U_{gh}=\sqrt{\frac{1+c_{gh}}2}\,I_2
-\frac{i}{\sqrt{2(1+c_{gh})}}(v_{gh}\cdot\sigma),
\quad c_{gh}=n_g\cdot n_h,\quad v_{gh}=n_g\times n_h,
\]
use the convention
\(U_{gh}(x\cdot\sigma)U_{gh}^{\dagger}=(R_{gh}x)\cdot\sigma\).
With this fixed formula the antipodal equality of the SU(2) links is
direct. Independently chosen signs for edge lifts would require a
separate compatibility check.

## 3. Path and plaquette holonomy

Because the link equality holds factor by factor, the ordered transports
of every path and every cycle agree with those of its antipodal image.
This is stronger than a statement about the 240 Q6 square faces. The
general proof and the precise product-order convention are in T143-A.

The previously recorded reconstructed-connection plaquette statistics
are
\[
\bar\theta=0.6692474402132716,\quad
\theta_{\rm RMS}=1.0062929279515105,\quad
\theta_{\max}=3.020912654710446.
\]
These belong to the reconstructed connection; they are not automatically
statistics of the original archived \`U_SU2\` array.

## 4. Consequence for a global complex phase

The reconstructed plaquette holonomies are non-Abelian: 22,248 of the
28,680 unordered pairs fail to commute modulo the centre of Spin(3).
Thus the full fundamental SU(2) holonomy family has no common invariant
complex line. A single global U(1) phase line cannot be selected as a
common eigenline of all these holonomies.

This obstructs interpreting the 29 numbers as phases of one globally
parallel line selected by this connection. It does not rule out a
different construction, such as a chosen reduction of structure group,
a gauge-covariant section with transition functions, or a path-dependent
phase. Each requires additional geometric data.

## 5. Anti-Twin quotient

For Q6 and \(p(g)=g\oplus7\), there are 32 vertex orbits. Under the
hypotheses of T143-A, the canonical SO(3) and SU(2) links descend
unambiguously to the quotient graph. This is a discrete graph quotient;
no manifold, flatness, or continuum assertion is made.
