# T143-C — Clifford symmetry and even spectral multiplicity of the fibre operator

**Classification:** conditional algebraic theorem; application to the archived \(K_{\rm fibre}\) is numerically certified to machine precision in T143-B.  
**No claim:** this theorem does not identify the generator provenance of the archived matrix.

## 1. Setup

Let
\[
\mathcal H=\mathbb C^{64}\otimes\mathbb C^2
\]
with basis \(\{|g,s\rangle:g\in\mathbb F_2^6,\ s\in\{0,1\}\}\), ordered by the interleaved index \(2g+s\). Let \(T_a|g\rangle=|g\oplus a\rangle\) be the bit-translation operator and let \(\sigma_1,\sigma_2,\sigma_3\) be the Pauli matrices.

Define
\[
G_1=T_1\otimes\sigma_3,\qquad
G_2=T_2\otimes\sigma_2,\qquad
G_3=T_4\otimes\sigma_1.
\tag{1}
\]
Let
\[
P=T_7\otimes I_2.
\tag{2}
\]

## 2. Algebraic relations

The translations \(T_1,T_2,T_4\) commute, are Hermitian involutions, and satisfy \(T_1T_2T_4=T_7\). The Pauli matrices are Hermitian involutions and pairwise anticommute. Therefore
\[
G_j^\dagger=G_j,\qquad G_j^2=I,
\tag{3}
\]
\[
G_iG_j+G_jG_i=0\quad(i\ne j).
\tag{4}
\]
Moreover, since \(\sigma_3\sigma_2\sigma_1=-iI_2\),
\[
G_1G_2G_3=-iP,\qquad
P=iG_1G_2G_3.
\tag{5}
\]
In particular \(P^2=I\), and \(P\) commutes with each \(G_j\), because moving one generator through the product of the other two produces two sign changes.

## 3. Theorem: parity reduction and even multiplicity

Let \(K=K^\dagger\) be an operator on \(\mathcal H\) satisfying
\[
[K,G_j]=0\qquad(j=1,2,3).
\tag{6}
\]
Then:

1. \(K\) commutes with the Anti-Twin involution \(P\).
2. The Hilbert space decomposes into invariant parity sectors
   \[
   \mathcal H=\mathcal H_+\oplus\mathcal H_-,
   \qquad
   \mathcal H_\pm=\ker(P\mp I).
   \tag{7}
   \]
   Since \(P=T_7\otimes I_2\) is fixed-point-free on the 64 vertex labels, each sector has dimension 64.
3. Every eigenvalue of \(K|_{\mathcal H_+}\) and every eigenvalue of \(K|_{\mathcal H_-}\) has even complex multiplicity.

**Proof.**

From (5) and (6),
\[
[K,P]=i[K,G_1G_2G_3]=0,
\]
which proves the first two assertions.

On \(\mathcal H_\pm\), equation (5) fixes the central Clifford volume element \(iG_1G_2G_3\) to the scalar \(\pm1\). The complex Clifford algebra generated by three anticommuting involutions, after fixing this central value, is isomorphic to \(M_2(\mathbb C)\). Its irreducible modules are therefore two-dimensional. Each eigenspace of \(K\) is invariant under all \(G_j\), by (6), and hence is a module over this matrix algebra. Its complex dimension must be a multiple of two. \(\square\)

The theorem does **not** imply equality of the spectra in the two different parity sectors. It proves even multiplicity separately within each sector.

## 4. Numerical application to the original fibre archive

T143-B uses the natural interleaved action
\[
P_{\rm fibre}|g,s\rangle=|g\oplus7,s\rangle
\]
on the archived \(128\times128\) matrix \(K_{\rm fibre}\). It also tests the three operators (1) directly.

The observed commutator Frobenius residuals are
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|[K_{\rm fibre},G_1]\|_F&=6.7704\times10^{-15},\\
\|[K_{\rm fibre},G_2]\|_F&=5.4892\times10^{-15},\\
\|[K_{\rm fibre},G_3]\|_F&=6.5383\times10^{-15}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The generator square, Hermiticity, pairwise anticommutator, and volume-relation residuals are zero in the stored floating-point computation.

The two parity blocks each have 64 dimensions. Numerically, each block has 32 distinct eigenvalues, each of multiplicity two. The sorted spectra of the two different parity blocks also agree to \(1.2435\times10^{-14}\), but that cross-sector isospectrality is an additional observation and is not a consequence of the theorem above.

## 5. Evidence boundary and open question

The algebraic theorem is exact. The archived application is currently a numerical certificate. A generator specification or exact symbolic construction of \(K_{\rm fibre}\) is needed to promote the three commutation identities from numerical observations to exact archive-level hypotheses.

The equality of the \(+\) and \(-\) spectra may indicate an additional symmetry beyond the three-generator Clifford algebra. It is deliberately left open rather than attributed to an unproved mechanism.

No conclusion is drawn for \(K_{\rm fibre,biverso}\), \(K_0\), or the tangent-space operators.
