# MQ-T003 — Invariant one-link gluing of two quantum tetrahedra

**Status:** proved analytically under the stated representation and orientation hypotheses.

## Statement

Let \(\iota_A\) and \(\iota_B\) be SU(2)-invariant four-valent tensors.
If one leg of each carries dual-equivalent representations and the two legs
are contracted with the invariant SU(2) pairing, the resulting tensor on the
six remaining legs is SU(2)-invariant.

## Proof

The tensor-product action leaves each intertwiner invariant. The invariant
pairing on the two contracted representations intertwines the group action
with the trivial representation. Contracting those legs therefore removes
the paired representation action while preserving invariance on the six
external legs. This is precisely closure of intertwiners under invariant
contraction. \(\square\)

## Spin-1/2 normalization corollary

For normalized four-valent invariant tensors in \((\mathbb C^2)^{\otimes4}\),
each one-leg reduced density matrix is \(I_2/2\), by SU(2) invariance and
Schur's lemma. Contracting two exposed legs with the normalized singlet
\((|01\rangle-|10\rangle)/\sqrt2\) gives a six-leg tensor of norm \(1/2\).
The factor 2 normalizes the resulting boundary state.

## Geometric interpretation

A shared spin label matches the quantum area spectrum on the glued face.
It does not impose equality of all face-shape observables. Area matching and
shape matching are distinct conditions.

## Exclusions

This theorem does not prove a global Regge geometry, a continuum limit, a
complete spinfoam dynamics, or an RGS/Q6 identification.
