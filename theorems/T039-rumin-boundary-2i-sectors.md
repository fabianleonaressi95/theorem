# T039 — Boundary sectors of the Rumin middle operator and the 2I restriction

**Status:** exact boundary eigenvalue bookkeeping; conditional finite-group decomposition interface. The geometric identification of each exceptional boundary module with a particular Sym^m(C^2) is not claimed.

## 1. Input from T025

T025 separates the generic region p,q >= 1 from two exceptional cases:

- q=-1, p>=1: the boundary type lies in the (1,0) component and lambda_q(p)=(p+1)^2/4.
- p=-1, q>=1: the conjugate boundary type lies in the (0,1) component and lambda_p(q)=(q+1)^2/4.

These are eigenvalues of D-adjoint-D on the stated boundary sectors (and T025 states the same values for the middle Rumin Laplacian there). They are not obtained by substituting a negative index into the generic formula (2pq+p+q)^2/4.

## 2. Exact boundary audit

For either boundary orientation, write n>=1 for the remaining positive index. Then lambda_boundary(n)=(n+1)^2/4 is rational and positive. The implementation uses exact SymPy rationals and rejects invalid labels or indices. The two orientations remain distinct labels even though their scalar eigenvalue functions agree.

## 3. Restriction to 2I: conditional, not inferred

The generic T025 block carries Sym^p+q(C^2) under SU(2), and T037 computes the exact decomposition of each supplied symmetric power under 2I.

For the boundary cases, T025 identifies the exceptional U(2)-type and scalar eigenvalue, but the cited boundary statement alone does not identify its SU(2) highest weight as a particular m. T039 therefore does not set m=p-1, m=p+1, or any other guessed relation.

The audit accepts an explicit representation_degree=m only as a conditional input. When supplied, it reuses T037's exact intertwiner/projector decomposition and verifies sum_rho d_rho b_(m,rho)=m+1. This verifies the finite-group decomposition of the selected symmetric power; it does not prove that this symmetric power is the geometric boundary module.
