# M3 — D4 orbit closure of the RGS/Q6 seed

**Status:** exact finite action on the 64 labelled seed points and exact graph-orbit count. The square-cell closure is an executable audit target; regularity, its face count, and its homology remain open until the audit is run and its output is reviewed. No continuum or physical chirality claim is made.

## 1. Six-bit action

Use the big-endian label
\[
v=(b_1,b_2,b_3\mid s_1,s_2,s_3)\in\{0,1\}^6.
\]
Define
\[
R(v)=(b_1,b_2,b_3\oplus b_1\oplus b_2\mid
s_2,1\oplus s_1,s_3),
\]
and
\[
S(v)=(b_1,b_2,b_3\mid
1\oplus s_1,s_2,s_3).
\]
These are permutations of the 64 labels. Direct substitution gives
\[
R^4=S^2=I,\qquad SRS=R^{-1}.
\]
The eight transformations generated by \(R,S\) are distinct, so the action is faithful and isomorphic to \(D_4\).

The square of the rotation is
\[
R^2(v)=(b_1,b_2,b_3\mid
1\oplus s_1,1\oplus s_2,s_3),
\]
i.e. XOR mask 6. The reflection \(S\) is XOR mask 4.

## 2. Graph orbit

For a difference vector \(d\in\mathbb F_2^6\), let
\[
E_d=\{\{v,v+d\}:v\in\mathbb F_2^6\}.
\]
Each nonzero direction gives 32 undirected edges. The linear parts of the eight group elements send the six standard coordinate directions to exactly
\[
\{e_1,e_2,e_3,e_4,e_5,e_6,
e_1+e_3,e_2+e_3\}.
\]
These eight directions are distinct. Hence the graph generated by the D4-orbit of the Q6 edges has exactly
\[
|V|=64,\qquad |E|=8\cdot32=256,
\]
and degree 8. The two additional edge directions are diagonals relative to the original Q6 coordinate graph.

This graph count does **not** by itself establish a regular square complex.

## 3. Generated square cells

Let \(\mathcal F_0\) be the 240 elementary square cycles of Q6. Define the finite family
\[
\mathcal F_{D_4}=\{g(F):g\in D_4,\ F\in\mathcal F_0\},
\]
with duplicate cells identified only when their four vertices and cyclic boundary agree up to dihedral reorientation.

The audit in `audits/audit_minkowski_q6_d4_cell_closure.py` constructs:
- the orbit of vertices, edges, and square cycles;
- integer boundary matrices \(B_1,B_2\);
- the induced signed permutation maps for every element of \(D_4\);
- pairwise square-intersection checks.

A pair of distinct squares is accepted as a regular square-complex intersection only when it is empty or a single common face (a vertex or a full common edge). Shared diagonals, three-vertex intersections, or the same four vertices with incompatible cyclic orders are reported as obstructions. The audit does not silently repair such intersections by subdividing cells.

## 4. Exact identities checked by the audit

For each \(g\in D_4\), the signed cell maps \(J_k(g)\) must satisfy
\[
J_0(g)B_1=B_1J_1(g),\qquad
J_1(g)B_2=B_2J_2(g),
\]
and the group relations on all three chain spaces. The boundary identity
\[
B_1B_2=0
\]
is checked entrywise over the integers.

The audit reports the number of distinct square cells, incompatible face identifications, non-regular face intersections, boundary-matrix dimensions, and chain-map/group-relation residuals. These outputs are deliberately not stated here as numerical results before execution.

## 5. Classification

**Proved algebraically:** faithful D4 action on the 64 labels; the relations \(R^4=S^2=I\), \(SRS=R^{-1}\); the XOR identities for \(R^2\) and \(S\); the 256-edge graph orbit and its eight directions.

**To be established computationally:** the exact number of distinct square cells; regularity of their intersections; the induced chain representation and its exact boundary identities; homology and Hodge spectrum.

**Not claimed:** that this orbit complex is already a regular CW complex, that its homology has any particular Betti numbers, or that the finite action proves a Lorentzian continuum limit or a physical chirality.

## 7. Liouville–Bergman connection

The exact conditional invariance lemma and its limitations are recorded separately in
`docs/Q6_D5_D4_LIOUVILLE_BRIDGE.md`. It proves that an orthogonal five-dimensional
representation of D4 acting diagonally on canonical coordinates `(q, pi)`
preserves the Liouville measure and the type-IV Bergman density
`N(z)^(-5) dmu_L`.

It does **not** yet identify that representation with the one induced from
the M3 Q6 orbit complex. Such an identification requires the D4-equivariant
cell/cochain complex, its Hodge operator, and an explicit intertwiner on the
five-dimensional spectral sector. The original Q6 cubical complex is not
D4-invariant under the M3 generator R.


## 8. Comparison with the original Q6 coexact D5 sector

An exact rational-arithmetic audit has now compared the original
\(\ker(D_1^TD_1-12I)\subset\mathbb Q^{192}\) with the new M3 orbit-graph
five-mode sector. The natural signed edge transport acts on the 256-edge
orbit graph. Under this transport,
\[
\operatorname{rank}((I-EE^T)J_REV)=2,
\]
so the old Q6 five-mode sector is not preserved by the M3 rotation.
Moreover, the old Q6 sector has \(S\)-action \(-I_5\), whereas the M3
orbit-graph five-mode sector has \(S\)-spectrum \((-1,-1,+1,+1,+1)\).
The representations are inequivalent; no invertible intertwiner exists.

This does not affect the exact D4 action on the orbit graph or its own
five-dimensional eigenspace. It rules out the naive identification with
the previously constructed Q6-D5 quotient. See
\`docs/Q6_D5_D4_LIOUVILLE_BRIDGE.md\` and the exact audit
\`audits/audit_q6_d5_m3_d4_intertwiner_obstruction.py\`.
