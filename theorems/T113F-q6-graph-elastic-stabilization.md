# T113-F — Q6 edge elasticity as a derived-form stabilization test for the RGS T2u triplet

**Classification:** exact variational construction plus a reproducible numerical threshold calculation. The Q6 graph Dirichlet form is a specified constitutive ansatz; its coefficient is not claimed to be fixed by graph topology alone.

## 1. Start with the actual Q6 incidence form

Let \(D_0:C^0(Q_6)\to C^1(Q_6)\) be an oriented incidence matrix of the canonical six-cube, with 64 vertices and 192 edges. Then
\[
L_{Q_6}=D_0^TD_0=6I_{64}-A_{Q_6}.
\]
For a Cartesian displacement \(u=(u_1,\ldots,u_{64})\in\mathbb R^{192}\), the edge Dirichlet energy is
\[
E_{Q_6}[u]=\frac{\kappa}{2}\|(D_0\otimes I_3)u\|^2
=\frac{\kappa}{2}u^T(L_{Q_6}\otimes I_3)u,
\qquad \kappa\geq0.
\]
This is the canonical nearest-neighbour quadratic form of Q6. It is invariant under the automorphism group of the six-cube. It is **not** invariant under all 48 point-cloud \(O_h\) permutations: T113-D established that only 16/48 preserve the bare Q6 adjacency. We therefore do not impose a false full-\(O_h\) degeneracy on this Q6 elastic correction.

## 2. Restrict the form to the already reconstructed RGS triplet

Use the existing constrained tangent basis \(Q\in\mathbb R^{192\times188}\) and first RGS \(T_{2u}\) triplet \(V\in\mathbb R^{188\times3}\), \(V^TV=I\), from `reconstruct_rgs_t2u_triplet`. For triplet amplitudes \(a\in\mathbb R^3\), set \(u=QVa\). The restricted Q6 stiffness is
\[
M_{Q_6}^{(T)}=
V^TQ^T(L_{Q_6}\otimes I_3)QV.
\]
The restricted Coulomb stiffness is
\[
K_T=V^TK_CV\simeq-0.06994933008401777 I_3.
\]
The combined quadratic potential is therefore
\[
E^{(2)}_T(a)=\frac12a^T(K_T+\kappa M_{Q_6}^{(T)})a.
\]
No scalar \(\mu I\) is inserted by hand. The Q6 term has a specified edge-by-edge origin, and its projection can split the threefold triplet because the Q6 adjacency is not invariant under the full induced \(O_h\) action.

## 3. Stability threshold — generalized eigenvalue formula

If \(M_{Q_6}^{(T)}\) is positive definite, strict harmonic stability is equivalent to
\[
K_T+\kappa M_{Q_6}^{(T)}\succ0.
\]
The sharp threshold is
\[
\boxed{\kappa_c=
\lambda_{\max}\!\left(-K_T,M_{Q_6}^{(T)}\right)}
\]
where the right side denotes the largest generalized eigenvalue of
\[
-K_Tx=\kappa_c M_{Q_6}^{(T)}x.
\]
Thus all three triplet polarizations are stable for \(\kappa>\kappa_c\), marginal directions occur at equality, and at least one remains unstable below the threshold. If the projected Q6 form is only semidefinite, the test must instead inspect the negative eigenspace of \(K_T\) on \(\ker M_{Q_6}^{(T)}\); a negative direction there cannot be stabilized by this edge term at any finite \(\kappa\).

The numeric \(\kappa_c\) is deliberately generated by the audit script from the archived seed and existing triplet reconstruction, rather than copied as an unverified constant into this theorem record.

## 4. Dynamics and dispersion boundary

With positive inertia matrix \(M_T^{\rm in}\), the finite triplet dynamics is
\[
M_T^{\rm in}\ddot a+
(K_T+\kappa M_{Q_6}^{(T)})a=0.
\]
Its squared frequencies are the generalized eigenvalues
\[
(K_T+\kappa M_{Q_6}^{(T)})x
=\omega^2M_T^{\rm in}x.
\]
This is a three-mode finite-dimensional oscillator. It is not yet a spatial dispersion relation \(\omega(k)\): Q6 supplies a finite graph and no continuum wave-vector without an additional embedding/refinement and reconstruction theorem.

## 5. What this does and does not derive

- **Derived from Q6 incidence:** the positive-semidefinite edge Dirichlet form and its exact Hessian \(L_{Q_6}\otimes I_3\).
- **Derived from the archived RGS triplet:** its restricted matrix \(M_{Q_6}^{(T)}\), computed without choosing a scalar shift.
- **Conditional constitutive input:** \(\kappa\), the energy scale assigned to a Q6 edge displacement. Topology fixes the form, not its physical units or numerical value.
- **Not established:** a phason observable, a second internal triplet, a material density law, a continuum sound speed, or a gravitational packet mass.
- **Symmetry warning:** do not call the Q6 correction fully \(O_h\)-invariant. The bare Q6 graph has only 16 of the 48 induced point-cloud signed-permutation symmetries.
