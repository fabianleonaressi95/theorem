# T027 — Exact McKay–Molien bridge for the nine Rumin twists

**Status:** exact coefficientwise identity, checked with integer arithmetic. This closes the representation-label and multiplicity bridge between the affine-E₈ recurrence in T026 and the nine Molien covariants used by T021–T023. In particular, T026's spinorial four-dimensional node is T021's \(4'\), not its nonspinorial \(4\). This map is fixed by occurrence degrees and central parity, not dimension alone.

## Coefficientwise identity

T026 derives the exact recurrence \(n(0)=e_1\), \(n(1)=e_{2_s}\), \(n(m+1)=A n(m)-n(m-1)\). For the matched T021 label \(\rho\), the covariant series is
\[
M_\rho(t)=\sum_{m\ge0}b_{m,\rho}t^m
=\frac{N_\rho(t)}{(1-t^{12})(1-t^{20})}.
\]
The audit verifies \(n_i(m)=b_{m,\rho(i)}\) for all nine irreducibles and every degree through the selected cutoff. It compares two independent constructions: the affine McKay representation-ring recurrence and the finite-group Molien covariants. Expansion uses exact integer convolution, not floating-point character values.

## Consequence for Rumin spectral multiplicities

For generic \(p,q\ge1\), T025 gives
\[
\lambda_{p,q}=\frac{(2pq+p+q)^2}{4},\qquad m=p+q.
\]
Its \(2I\)-isotypic multiplicity \(n_i(m)\) is therefore exactly the T021 coefficient \(b_{m,\rho(i)}\). The McKay and Molien descriptions assign the same multiplicity to every generic T025 positive block in all nine twists. This links T021's paired series and T022's eventual Hurwitz decomposition to the independent T026 recurrence; T023's Gamma-reflection reduction acts on those same covariant multiplicities.

**Scope:** this is a multiplicity identity, not an independent derivation of the full Rumin zeta combination, exceptional boundary families, grading weights, or determinant-line convention. Those remain governed by T021–T024. The T025 boundary families are not generated by substituting negative indices into the generic formula.
