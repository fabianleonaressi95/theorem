# T080 — Exact discrete Stokes complex and structured-gradient incompatibility on the Q-ball shell

**Status:** exact for the generated finite triangulated shell. This is a finite-mesh theorem, not a continuum relaxation or homogenization theorem.

## 1. Canonical oriented triangulation

Let \(\mathcal T\) be the centre-fan triangulation generated by \`models/football_shell.py::build_football_shell\`. It has 60 physical vertices, 32 panel-centre vertices, 92 triangulation vertices, 32 panels, 180 oriented triangles and 270 edges. The 90 physical panel seams are a distinguished subset of the 270 triangulation edges.

Orient every triangle consistently outward. Orient each edge by increasing vertex index. Define the integer incidence matrices
\[
B_1\in\mathbb Z^{92\times270},\qquad
B_2\in\mathbb Z^{270\times180}.
\]
The column of \(B_1\) for edge \(e=(i,j)\), \(i<j\), is \(-e_i+e_j\). The column of \(B_2\) for an oriented triangle is its signed edge boundary.

## 2. Chain-complex identity

For each oriented triangle, its three oriented edges have zero boundary. Therefore
\[
\boxed{B_1B_2=0}
\]
as an exact integer identity, not a floating-point approximation.

The primal graph is connected and the dual face graph is connected; every edge has exactly two incident triangles with opposite induced orientations. Hence
\[
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}B_1=92-1=91,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}B_2=180-1=179.
\]
Consequently
\[
\dim H_1(\mathcal T;\mathbb Q)
=270-91-179=0,
\]
and
\[
\chi=92-270+180=2.
\]
The panel-centre vertices are a triangulation device; they do not change the topology of the closed shell. The 90 seam edges do not create 90 independent homology cycles.

## 3. Discrete Stokes theorem

Let \(d\in\mathbb R^{270\times3}\) be any vector-valued edge 1-cochain. Its face curl is
\[
(\operatorname{curl}_h d):=B_2^Td\in\mathbb R^{180\times3}.
\]
For any integer face 2-chain \(q\in\mathbb Z^{180}\), its oriented boundary edge chain is \(B_2q\). Then
\[
\boxed{
(B_2q)^Td=q^T(B_2^Td).
}
\]
This is the discrete Stokes identity, componentwise in \(\mathbb R^3\), and follows directly from matrix transposition. It is exact for every edge cochain and every face chain.

## 4. Structured-gradient mismatch

Let \(y:\mathcal V\to\mathbb R^3\) be a nodal macroscopic deformation. Its edge coboundary is
\[
(\delta_0y)_e=y_j-y_i,\qquad e=(i,j),\ i<j.
\]
Let \(G\in\mathbb R^{270\times3}\) be a microscopic edge 1-cochain, and define the discrete structured mismatch
\[
\mathsf D_h:=\delta_0y-G.
\]
Since \(B_2^T\delta_0y=0\) (equivalently, the sum of nodal differences around every triangle vanishes),
\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{curl}_h\mathsf D_h
=B_2^T\mathsf D_h
=-B_2^TG.
}
\]
Thus the discrete incompatibility is the negative curl of the microscopic edge field in this convention. A nonzero value is a genuine cochain incompatibility; it is not inferred merely from the existence of a physical seam.

For a surface structured-deformation model, \(G\) must be obtained by a declared projection/integration of the microscopic tangential gradient along each oriented edge. The algebraic theorem does not choose that constitutive reconstruction.

## 5. Seam traces and disarrangements

The canonical continuous triangulation has shared edge degrees of freedom. To represent independent panel traces, use the existing duplicated-panel construction \`build_seam_discontinuous_mesh\`. Each physical seam then has two traces and a chart/transport identification. A seam jump is a separate trace observable. It must be computed after applying the correct panel-frame or bundle transition; raw coordinate subtraction is not invariant under independent chart changes.

Accordingly:
- the 90 seams are an interface set;
- a seam disarrangement requires a nonzero covariantly compared trace mismatch;
- a bulk cochain curl and a seam jump are different measures;
- no seam damage, dislocation or fatigue law follows from \(B_1B_2=0\).

## 6. Exact finite-mesh result versus Morandotti's continuum theory

This theorem establishes the incidence complex and discrete Stokes identity on one finite shell mesh. It supplies the algebraic compatibility layer needed for a Morandotti-type discrete structured-deformation energy.

It does **not** prove:
- a surface version of Morandotti's Euclidean relaxation theorem;
- equicoercivity, a liminf inequality or a recovery sequence;
- \(\Gamma\)- or Mosco-convergence under mesh refinement;
- convergence of discrete seam jumps to a continuum disarrangement measure;
- a constitutive coupling to the left Bernoulli-gradient field or the right gauge-covariant field.

Those require a shape-regular refinement family, compatible bundle/panel charts, a specified edge reconstruction of \(G\), bulk/interface energy hypotheses, and independent convergence estimates.
