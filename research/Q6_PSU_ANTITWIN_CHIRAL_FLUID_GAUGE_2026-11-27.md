# Q6–PSU–Anti-Twin: right/left fluid gauge and spinor locking

**Date:** 27 November 2026  
**Classification:** finite algebraic identities audited; continuum matter model proposed with explicit unresolved action gates.

The doubled carrier is \(Q6\times\{+,-\}\): 64 hexagrams per sheet, 128 total. The exchange is \(J=I_4\otimes\tau_1\), and the charge operator is \(Q=q_0\tau_3\), so \(JQJ^{-1}=-Q\). The Anti-Twin link is the conjugate representation \(U_-=U_+^*\); for compact \(U(1)\), this is the inverse link.

The right/left assignment is imposed by the locking operator
\[
\mathscr C_{RL}=\gamma^5\otimes\tau_3,\qquad
P_{\rm lock}=\frac{1+\mathscr C_{RL}}2.
\]
It retains \(P_R\otimes P_+\) and \(P_L\otimes P_-\): one Dirac spinor split across PSU and Anti-Twin sheets. Bare sheet exchange flips the lock; parity combined with sheet exchange preserves it.

Retain the fluid variables
\[
\Phi_\pm=\ln N_\pm,\quad n_\pm=\mathcal A_\pm/N_\pm,\quad
\xi_\pm=(n_\pm\cdot n_\pm)\Phi_\pm.
\]
For each variable \(X\), \(X_e=(X_++X_-)/2\), \(X_o=(X_+-X_-)/2\). The sheet connections are \(a_\pm=a_e\pm a_o\), and the locked spinor sees
\[
D_\mu=\nabla_\mu+iq(a_{e\mu}+a_{o\mu}\gamma^5).
\]
Thus \(a_e\) is vector/diagonal and \(a_o\) is axial/relative. In the conjugate Anti-Twin limit \(a_-=-a_+\), the vector part vanishes and the connection is purely relative/axial.

The PSU scale is \(\ell_{\rm PSU}=\lambda_{\rm PSU}\ell_P\), \(r_{\rm PSU}=\ell_{\rm PSU}/2\), \(V_{\rm PSU}=\pi\ell_{\rm PSU}^3/6\), \(A_{\rm eq}=\pi\ell_{\rm PSU}^2/4\). Retain \(\alpha_G=GM^2/(\hbar c)=(M/M_P)^2\), \(q_0=\sqrt{\alpha_G}\). This is a model normalization, not a measured coupling. T146's no-overlap bound is \(\lambda_\parallel\ge2.5129180853\ell_P\) in physical cut-project coordinates.

The minimal common action has a symmetrized Cartan-covariant Dirac term and separate Maxwell terms for \(f_e=da_e\) and \(f_o=da_o\). A bare Dirac mass breaks the local relative axial gauge symmetry. A massive phase needs an inter-sheet bridge \(\Delta\) with \(\Delta\mapsto e^{2iq\chi_o}\Delta\), or else the phase is massless. The previously defined relative modulus \(\rho\) does not by itself provide this phase.

The finite audit checks the Minkowski Clifford identities, rank-four locking projector, right/left sheet decomposition, parity-plus-exchange invariance, charge conjugation under sheet exchange, vector/axial gauge reduction, conjugate link covariance, fluid even/odd variables, and PSU volume/area normalization.

This does not prove a continuum Q6 Dirac spectrum or evade Nielsen–Ninomiya; the two sheets instead form a vector-like Dirac pair. The 64 tetrahedron/Q6 match remains a candidate bipartition labeling until cell coordinates, incidence and symmetry actions are supplied. The fluid constitutive action and any Q-ball/Higgs phase remain to be derived.
