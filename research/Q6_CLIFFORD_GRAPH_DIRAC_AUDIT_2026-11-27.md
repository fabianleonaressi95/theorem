# Q6 Clifford graph operator — spectral diagnostic

The Q6 first-order Clifford graph operator is \(D_{Q6}=\frac12\sum_{i=1}^6K_i\otimes\Gamma_i\), where \(K_i=T_i-T_i^\dagger\) is the oriented partial shift on the 32 disjoint edges in bit direction \(i\). The six \(K_i\) commute and square to \(-I\); the Cl(6) generators anticommute. Hence \(D_{Q6}^\dagger=-D_{Q6}\), \(\{\Gamma_*,D_{Q6}\}=0\), and \(D_{Q6}^2=-3I/2\) exactly.

The 512-dimensional operator has only two eigenvalues, \(\pm i\sqrt{3/2}\), each with multiplicity 256. It has no zero mode or low-energy Dirac cone. The link-twisted operator remains anti-Hermitian, chiral and locally gauge-covariant; the Anti-Twin sheet carries the conjugate link representation. This is a finite diagnostic operator, not a successful physical fermion discretization. A refinement-consistent/domain-wall/overlap construction remains necessary.
