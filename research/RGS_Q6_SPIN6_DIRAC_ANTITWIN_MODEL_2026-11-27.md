## 1. Six-dimensional Euclidean module

The golden cut-and-project frame from T129 is an element \(O_6\in SO(6)\). It therefore admits two lifts to Spin(6), differing by sign. A concrete complex Clifford module is generated by
\[
\begin{aligned}
\Gamma_1&=\sigma_1\otimes I\otimes I,&
\Gamma_2&=\sigma_2\otimes I\otimes I,\\
\Gamma_3&=\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_1\otimes I,&
\Gamma_4&=\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_2\otimes I,\\
\Gamma_5&=\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_1,&
\Gamma_6&=\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_3\otimes\sigma_2.
\end{aligned}
\]
These are Hermitian \(8\times8\) matrices with \(\{\Gamma_I,\Gamma_J\}=2\delta_{IJ}\). The chirality matrix is \(\Gamma_*=i\Gamma_1\cdots\Gamma_6\), with rank-four eigenspaces.

For the active plane rotation \(e_I\mapsto\cos\theta\,e_I+\sin\theta\,e_J\), the spin lift is
\[
S_{IJ}(\theta)=\cos(\theta/2)I_8-\sin(\theta/2)\Gamma_I\Gamma_J.
\]
It is unitary, acts on the Clifford generators by the corresponding SO(6) rotation, and changes sign at \(2\pi\), returning at \(4\pi\).

This is a representation statement; it does not determine a coupling constant or turn the internal 3-space into time.

## 3. Lorentzian reduction and Cartan torsion

\[
D_\mu=\partial_\mu+\frac14\omega_{\mu AB}\gamma^{AB}-iqA_\mu.
\]
The connection may have both curvature and torsion. A teleparallel representative is the separate flat-connection branch. The torsion–spin-current relation depends on the selected action; it is not inserted by analogy.
