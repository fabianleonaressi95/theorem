# Case SGC-003 — Equal-radius tangency model

**Status:** MODEL / EXACT-COMPUTATION; historical attribution unverified.

For
\[
r_1=(r^2-x^2)/(2r),\quad
r_3=-r_1+\sqrt{r_1^2+x^2},\quad
r_4=(r^2+x^2)/(2r)-r_1,
\]
the positive-root identity gives
\[
r_3=r_4=x^2/r.
\]
At x/r=1/3, r1/r=4/9 and r3/r=r4/r=1/9.

This sheet certifies only the algebra written above. It does not certify that the equations encode all tangencies in a specific 1837 Aichi Sangaku. The primary diagram and internal/external tangency conditions must be verified before historical identification.
