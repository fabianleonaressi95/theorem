# Case SGC-002 — Regular pentagon

**Status:** EXACT for the abstract regular pentagon; any particular Sangaku construction needs its own transcription.

\[
d/l=\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2,\qquad \varphi^2=\varphi+1,
\]
\[
l^2=(5-\sqrt5)R^2/2,\quad d^2=(5+\sqrt5)R^2/2.
\]
For R=1, squared characteristic distances generate Q(sqrt(5)). Complex vertices generate Q(zeta_5), degree four. Real Cartesian coordinates lie in Q(zeta_20), degree eight, while their real field is Q(sqrt(5),sqrt(10+2sqrt(5))), degree four.

The geometric symmetry group is D5 (order 10); Gal(Q(zeta_5)/Q) is C4. These groups are not identified.
