# Spherical-space-form source literature

- [18_57.pdf](18_57.pdf) — Akira Ikeda, “On the Spectrum of Homogeneous Spherical Space Forms,” *Kodai Mathematical Journal* 18 (1995), 57–67.

The paper is a primary source for Poincaré series and Laplace spectra of homogeneous spherical space forms associated with finite groups. The repository's theorem-level reconstructions and scope limitations are recorded in [S011](../../../theorems/S011-ikeda-binary-tetrahedral-spectrum.md), [S012](../../../theorems/S012-ikeda-cyclic-binary-polyhedral-spectra.md) and [S013](../../../theorems/S013-general-spherical-space-form-molien-diagonal-actions.md).
