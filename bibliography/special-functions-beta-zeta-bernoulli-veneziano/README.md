# Special functions, moments and Morandotti — bibliography

**Compiled:** 10 November 2026  
**Purpose:** primary and reference sources for the Gamma–Beta–digamma–zeta–Bernoulli chain, simplex/Dirichlet moments, Veneziano Beta amplitudes, structured deformations and incompatible elasticity with defects.

Status labels: **VERIFIED** = bibliographic metadata checked against publisher/institutional/primary records; **REFERENCE** = authoritative reference work; **PREPRINT** = not to be described as peer-reviewed unless subsequently published.

## A. Gamma, Beta, polygamma, zeta and Bernoulli

1. **NIST Digital Library of Mathematical Functions (DLMF)**, Chapters 5, 24 and 25, current online edition.  
   - Chapter 5, *Gamma Function*: definitions and functional relations; §5.5 reflection and multiplication identities; §5.11 asymptotic expansions; §5.12 Beta function; §5.14 multidimensional integrals; §5.15 polygamma functions.  
   - Chapter 24, *Bernoulli and Euler Polynomials*: generating functions, identities and values.  
   - Chapter 25, *Zeta and Related Functions*: Riemann zeta identities and special values.  
   - Chapter 2 §2.10(i), Euler–Maclaurin formula.  
   URLs: https://dlmf.nist.gov/5 ; https://dlmf.nist.gov/24 ; https://dlmf.nist.gov/25 ; https://dlmf.nist.gov/2.10  
   **Status:** REFERENCE. Main authority for the special-function identities and asymptotic expansions used in S.3 and the synthesis document.

2. **Abramowitz, M.; Stegun, I. A. (eds.)**, *Handbook of Mathematical Functions with Formulas, Graphs, and Mathematical Tables*, National Bureau of Standards Applied Mathematics Series 55, 1964.  
   Digital edition and formula cross-references are incorporated in DLMF.  
   **Status:** REFERENCE.

3. **Dirichlet distribution and simplex moments.** The normalized simplex density and its Gamma-ratio moments are standard Dirichlet-distribution identities. For the present repository, the proof is given directly in T070/S.3; the DLMF multidimensional Gamma/Beta integral is the special-function reference.  
   **Status:** exact standard identity; no claim of a new probability distribution.

## B. Veneziano and Beta-function amplitudes

4. **Veneziano, G.**, “Construction of a crossing-symmetric, Regge-behaved amplitude for linearly rising trajectories,” *Il Nuovo Cimento A* **57** (1968), 190–197. DOI: [10.1007/BF02824451](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02824451). CERN record: https://cds.cern.ch/record/390478  
   **Status:** VERIFIED. Primary historical source for the Beta-function amplitude. Its use here documents the exact appearance of Euler Beta/Gamma functions in a scattering amplitude; it does not identify a shell phase integral with a string amplitude.

5. **Mandelstam, S.**, “Veneziano Formula with Trajectories Spaced by Two Units,” *Physical Review Letters* **21** (1968), 1724. DOI: [10.1103/PhysRevLett.21.1724](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.21.1724).  
   **Status:** VERIFIED. Related development of the Veneziano-type amplitude.

6. **Berger, E. L.; Fox, G. C.**, “Pion-Nucleon and Kaon-Nucleon Scattering in the Veneziano Model,” *Physical Review* **188** (1969), 2120. DOI: [10.1103/PhysRev.188.2120](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.188.2120).  
   **Status:** VERIFIED. Phenomenological use of amplitudes constructed from Beta-function terms.

## C. Structured deformations and disarrangements

7. **Amar, M.; Matias, J.; Morandotti, M.; Zappale, E.**, “Periodic homogenization in the context of structured deformations,” *Zeitschrift für angewandte Mathematik und Physik* **73** (2022), article 173. DOI: [10.1007/s00033-022-01817-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00033-022-01817-6).  
   Publisher: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00033-022-01817-6  
   **Status:** VERIFIED. Main source for periodic homogenization of first-order structured deformations and the relaxed bulk/interfacial integral representation. Its Euclidean domain and density hypotheses must be retained; it is not already a theorem for a closed curved football shell.

8. **Barroso, A. C.; Matias, J.; Morandotti, M.; Owen, D. R.**, “Explicit Formulas for Relaxed Disarrangement Densities Arising from Structured Deformations,” *Mathematics and Mechanics of Complex Systems* **5** (2017), no. 2, 163–189. DOI: [10.2140/memocs.2017.5.163](https://doi.org/10.2140/memocs.2017.5.163).  
   Institutional record: https://iris.polito.it/handle/11583/2722665 ; CVGMT: https://cvgmt.sns.it/paper/2776/  
   **Status:** VERIFIED. Source for the distinction M = grad g − G and the role of M and its curl in Burgers/dislocation descriptions. Do not identify the fully symmetric fourth moment in S.4 with torsion.

## D. Incompatible elasticity, Airy stress function and lattice defects

9. **Cesana, P.; De Luca, L.; Morandotti, M.**, “Semi-discrete modeling of systems of wedge disclinations and edge dislocations via the Airy stress function method,” *SIAM Journal on Mathematical Analysis* **56** (2024), no. 1, 79–136. DOI: [10.1137/22M1523443](https://doi.org/10.1137/22M1523443). Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02511  
   Publisher: https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/22M1523443 ; CVGMT: https://cvgmt.sns.it/paper/5618/  
   **Status:** VERIFIED. Core reference for the planar Airy variational treatment of wedge disclinations, disclination dipoles and edge dislocations.

10. **Cesana, P.; Fabbrini, E.; Morandotti, M.**, “Variational formulation of planar linearized elasticity with incompatible kinematics,” *Journal of Elasticity* **157** (2025), article 71. DOI: [10.1007/s10659-025-10161-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10659-025-10161-5). Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18053  
    CVGMT: https://cvgmt.sns.it/paper/7044/  
    **Status:** VERIFIED. Variational characterization for planar strain with incompatible kinematics and finite-dimensional reduction through cell formulas.

11. **Cesana, P.; Grillo, A.; Morandotti, M.; Pastore, A.**, “Dissipative evolution of Volterra disclinations,” *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* **85** (2025), 1361–1386. DOI: [10.1137/24M1688096](https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1688096). Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15457  
    **Status:** VERIFIED. Existence and dissipative evolution with crystalline preferred directions under the hypotheses of the paper.

12. **Cesana, P.; Fabbrini, E.; León Baldelli, A. A.; Morandotti, M.**, “Semidiscrete Modeling of Dislocation-Disclination Systems: Finite Element Formulation,” arXiv:2609.29614 (submitted 28 August 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29614  
    CVGMT: https://cvgmt.sns.it/paper/7600/  
    **Status:** PREPRINT (as catalogued 10 November 2026). Reference for the fourth-order Airy problem, CIP-DG discretization and finite family of cell problems. Do not label as peer-reviewed without an updated publication record.

## F. Citation discipline

- Cite DLMF for Gamma/Beta/polygamma, Bernoulli and zeta identities.
- Cite Veneziano (1968) only for the Beta-function scattering-amplitude construction.
- Cite Amar–Matias–Morandotti–Zappale (2022) for periodic structured-deformation homogenization under that paper's assumptions.
- Cite Barroso–Matias–Morandotti–Owen (2017) for the disarrangement field M = grad g − G and its curl interpretation.
- Cite Cesana et al. for planar incompatible elasticity/Airy and the specific finite-element formulations.
- Internal RGS/Q6 numerical observations must cite the internal theorem/audit and its data artifact; an external article does not certify those values.

## H. Q6 cubical topology, synchronization and finite-group representations

24. **Ghorbanchian, R.; Restrepo, J. G.; Torres, J. J.; Bianconi, G.**, “Higher-order simplicial synchronization of coupled topological signals,” *Communications Physics* **4**, article 120 (2021). DOI: [10.1038/s42005-021-00605-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-021-00605-4).  
    **Status:** VERIFIED. Source model for the node/link NL and NLT equations used in T071 (Eqs. 12–13 and 16–17). T071 replaces simplicial triangles with Q6 square faces; that cubical substitution is the present project's generalization, not a theorem from the article.

25. **Serre, J.-P.**, *Linear Representations of Finite Groups*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 42, Springer, 1977.  
    **Status:** REFERENCE. Character theory and restriction of finite-group representations used in the \(A_5\downarrow A_4\) and \(O_h\downarrow A_4\) comparison in S.7. The specific intertwiner matrix is not supplied by the reference or by the current record.

26. **Hatcher, A.**, *Algebraic Topology*, Cambridge University Press, 2002, especially §2.1.  
    **Status:** REFERENCE. Cellular homology and chain-complex background for the Q6 cubical 2-skeleton in T071. The finite Q6 incidence and rank statements are project-specific and are proved/audited in the repository.

27. **Johnson, N. L.; Kotz, S.; Balakrishnan, N.**, *Continuous Multivariate Distributions*, Vol. 1: *Models and Applications*, 2nd ed., Wiley, 1994.  
    **Status:** REFERENCE. Standard Dirichlet-distribution moments and multivariate-beta normalization underlying T070. The repository gives its own derivation for the triangular-cell normalization.
