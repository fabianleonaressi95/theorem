## External scientific controls

- Nitrogenase is an ATP-dependent metalloenzyme system involving Fe and MoFe protein components, electron/proton transfer, metal clusters and the FeMo cofactor. The 4D-cavity explanation is not supported by the cited reviews.
- Soap films and bubbles are variational systems under boundary/volume constraints. Minimal-surface form-finding is useful experimental mathematics, but it does not imply torsion.
- Molecular geometry must distinguish electron-domain geometry from molecular shape: water is bent despite tetrahedral electron-domain geometry; ammonia is trigonal pyramidal; methane, sulfate and phosphate have tetrahedral coordination geometries.

## Source access limitations

The In2Infinity pages were not directly openable by the page reader (403), but their indexed page text and FAQ were retrieved through web search. The Pinterest page was also inaccessible. The ResearchGate page exposed metadata and caption but not the full source publication. No statements beyond those retrieved are attributed to these pages.
