# Forensic provenance: (K_0), (P_G), and (T_{transport})

**Status:** matrix identities reconstructed; original generation of (K_0) and its 188D basis remains unresolved.

## 1. What is actually established

From the archived NPZ:
- (P_G) is the spectral projector onto the 64-dimensional numerical kernel of (K_0).
- (K_0P_G=P_GK_0\simeq0), (operatorname{rank}K_0=124), and (operatorname{rank}P_G=64).
- The positive spectrum of (K_0) is a ladder from 0.5 to 12.8 with spacing 0.1 to numerical precision.
- The archived (T_{transport}) satisfies
  [
  T_{transport}=U_{polar}A_{geometry}^T.
  ]
  It is a rank-64 partial isometry, with (T^TT=AA^T) and (TT^T=U_{polar}U_{polar}^T).
- (U_{polar}) is the polar factor of (P_GA_{geometry}). Thus the target 64D subspace is imposed by (P_G), and the transport is built after that projection.

Consequently (T_{transport}) is not an independently constructed connection: it is a polar transport from the chosen geometric feature frame to the spectral kernel frame.

## 2. Geometry-frame dependence

The archived feature frame is built from local modes proportional to
[
n_i=\frac{X_i\times a}{\|X_i\times a\|},
]
for a fixed generic axis (a), then projected into the tangent coordinates and orthonormalized.

A fixed axis is a gauge/frame choice. Under a spatial symmetry (T\in O_h), the feature construction is not automatically equivariant unless the axis is transformed as part of the data. The audit tests the actual 16 transformations preserving both the point cloud and Q6 adjacency. The feature map and its projected polar frame fail the full intertwining test.

## 3. Critical provenance gap in (K_0)

The recovered archive proves the spectral properties of (K_0), not its physical derivation. The associated source PDF contains notebook code with explicitly synthetic alternatives:
- a random Gram-matrix kernel (K_0=R^TR+0.1I);
- a mock 188D kernel builder;
- an illustrative spectral-mask construction of (P_G).

These snippets prove that synthetic test paths existed in the notebook; they do **not**, by themselves, prove that the exact archived (K_0) was generated by those particular cells. The exact generating cell, inputs, and coordinate-basis certificate are still missing.

Therefore:
- do not call the archived (K_0) a geometry-derived Hessian on the evidence currently recovered;
- do not call (P_G) an independently derived geometric projector: it is numerically the kernel spectral projector of (K_0);
- do not interpret (T_{transport}) as a canonical Q6 connection.

## 4. Important correction to the previous group-decomposition conclusion

The 188D representation (R_{geo,tangent}) is geometrically reconstructed from (Q_{tangent}). Its group decomposition is valid as a representation-theoretic statement.

However, applying that representation to test (K_0), (P_G), or (T_{transport}) requires proof that those matrices use exactly the same 188D coordinate basis. The archive lacks an independent basis-identification certificate. Hence the earlier statement that these operators break the order-16 symmetry is **conditional**, not an unconditional theorem. The numerical commutators are reproducible in the stored array coordinates, but their geometric interpretation depends on that missing basis identification.
