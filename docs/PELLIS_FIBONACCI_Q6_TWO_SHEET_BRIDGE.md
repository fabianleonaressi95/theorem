## 1. The two-sheet matching problem

For one sheet, let the transfer matrix at spectral parameter \(E\) be
\[
M(E)=\begin{pmatrix}a(E)&b(E)\\c(E)&d(E)\end{pmatrix},
\qquad \det M(E)=1.
\]
In the recovered identical-sheet neck matching convention, the Kirchhoff condition reduces to
\[
\boxed{2b(E)d(E)=0.}
\]
Thus the matching locus is the union of two algebraic channels:
\[
b(E)=0\quad\text{(antisymmetric channel)},\qquad
d(E)=0\quad\text{(symmetric channel)}.
\]
This factorization is a property of the stated neck matching problem. It must not be inferred from the trace alone: \(\operatorname{tr}M=a+d\) does not determine \(b\) or \(d\).

For an abstract pair of identical sheet state spaces \(\mathcal K_+\oplus\mathcal K_-\), the sheet exchange is
\[
\Sigma(\psi_+,\psi_-)=(\psi_-,\psi_+),\qquad
\Sigma^2=I,
\]
with projections \(\frac12(I\pm\Sigma)\). If the two uncoupled sheets carry the same transfer \(M(E)\), then \(M(E)\oplus M(E)\) commutes with \(\Sigma\). The identification of these exchange eigenspaces with the two factors \(b=0\) and \(d=0\) uses the specific neck boundary convention above; it is not a consequence of \(\det M=1\) alone.

## 2. Fibonacci transfer recursion and the trace map

Assume the radial/word hierarchy is generated by the Fibonacci concatenation, with unimodular transfer matrices
\[
M_{n+1}=M_nM_{n-1},\qquad M_n\in SL(2,\mathbb C),
\]
and compatible initial ordering, so that \(M_n=M_{n-1}M_{n-2}\) as well. Set
\[
x_n=\frac12\operatorname{tr}M_n.
\]
For \(A,B\in SL(2,\mathbb C)\), the Cayley–Hamilton trace identity gives
\[
\operatorname{tr}(A)\operatorname{tr}(B)
=\operatorname{tr}(AB)+\operatorname{tr}(AB^{-1}).
\]
Using \(M_{n-2}=M_{n-1}^{-1}M_n\) and cyclicity of trace yields
\[
\boxed{x_{n+1}=2x_nx_{n-1}-x_{n-2}.}
\]
The trace-map Jacobian is
\[
DT(x,y,z)=\begin{pmatrix}2y&2x&-1\\1&0&0\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det DT=-1.
\]
Thus the ambient determinant of a period-\(q\) monodromy is \((-1)^q\). On a regular Fricke level the normal multiplier is 1, so the two tangential multipliers have product \((-1)^q\). In particular, period-six tangential monodromy is reciprocal; period-three monodromy has negative determinant. The specific period-three orbit in T149 lies on a singular Fricke level, so its quoted spectrum is ambient, not a regular tangential spectrum.

Equivalently, the triple evolves under
\[
T(x,y,z)=(2xy-z,x,y).
\]
The Fricke polynomial
\[
I(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2+z^2-2xyz-1
\]
is invariant under \(T\). This is an exact statement about the trace coordinates of an \(SL(2)\) Fibonacci transfer cocycle. It does not reconstruct the individual matrix entries \(b_n,d_n\).

For two identical sheets following the same Fibonacci word, the two trace triples coincide before the neck boundary condition is imposed. The trace map therefore organizes the common bulk transfer hierarchy; the sheet channel is additional boundary/matching data.

## 5. The actual Pellis intertwining problem

The trace map retains only \(\frac12\operatorname{tr}M_n\). The neck matching condition depends on individual entries \(b_n,d_n\). Therefore a trace-orbit match is necessary structural information but is not sufficient for a Pellis spectral equivalence.

A valid next step must specify:
1. the full energy-dependent Pellis transfer state space and its boundary/neck domain;
2. the full matrix recursion on both sheets, including the precise multiplication order and any inter-sheet coupling;
3. the Q6-side operator or transfer object acting on the relevant invariant subspace (not an invented \(6\times6\) matrix inferred from \(C_6\));
4. a linear map \(\Phi_E\) between the actual state spaces, with compatible inner products and domains, satisfying the operator/transfer intertwining equation;
5. the sheet condition
   \[
   \Phi_E S_{\rm Pellis}=J\Phi_E
   \]
   on the subspace where the T150 orbit model applies;
6. preservation of the boundary matching factors \(b(E)=0\) and \(d(E)=0\), including multiplicities and spectral normalization.

Until these data are specified and the intertwining equations are checked, the Pellis correspondence is OPEN. The exact D6/Q6 orbit correspondence in T150 is a genuine bridge, but it is not yet the physical Pellis operator equivalence.
